Component tests and full-system runs are different checks.

Component tests
---------------
python -m tests.test_lateral_shift
6 passed

python -m tests.test_touchdown
6 passed

These tests check planner, inverse kinematics, the shift reference, the
touchdown observation, the residual clip, and the per-step cost used by the
Lagrange multiplier. They do not show that the robot completes a stable step.

Full-system runs
----------------
The standing push, one-step walk, multi-step schedule and touchdown-residual
rollouts are MuJoCo experiments. Their figures are in data/figures/. A passing
unit test does not mean the landing or a multi-step gait succeeded.
